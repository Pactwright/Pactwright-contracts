# ADR-011: Separate verdict and settle deadlines plus a silence policy

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-28)
- **Governs:** [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) §4.4, §5.4
- **Review items:** B2

## Context
A single expiry let anyone release the escrow after an ALLOW but before the seller settled.

## Decision
Each pact has `verdict_deadline` (last ledger for `record_verdict`) and `settle_deadline` (last ledger for `settle`). A silent verifier triggers the policy agreed at open: `Refund` (anyone may release) or `Pay` (the seller may settle up to the cap). Any open pact past `settle_deadline` is releasable to the buyer.

## Consequences
- A delivering seller cannot lose payment to an early release; funds are never stranded.
