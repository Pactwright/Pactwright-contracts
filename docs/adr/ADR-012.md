# ADR-012: Signed delivery receipts; the buyer submits evidence

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-28)
- **Governs:** [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) §5.1, §6.3
- **Review items:** B4

## Context
If the seller submits evidence and holds the refund key, the seller controls the outcome.

## Decision
Every paid response carries `X-Pactwright-Delivery`: the seller's ed25519 signature over `pact_id` and the body hash. The **buyer** submits the body and receipt to the verifier. No delivery receipt within the timeout means DENY.

## Consequences
- Neither side can fake what was delivered.
- M0 refunds still need the seller's cooperation (stated openly).
