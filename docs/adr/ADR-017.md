# ADR-017: M1 (`upto`) stays out of scope; P9-06 closes as dropped

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-31)
- **Governs:** [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) P9-06 · [PRD.md](../PRD.md) §13 and the source-project table · [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) "Deferred" · [ARCHITECTURE_ESSENTIALS.md](../ARCHITECTURE_ESSENTIALS.md) §4
- **Related:** ADR-001 (why M1 lost to M2), ADR-002 (superseded, and the pending question this closes)

## Context

P9-06 was written as a gate, not a task: "re-evaluate once Accensa ADR-002 resolves the `upto`
allowance design; new ADR either way." Leaving it `[ ]` means the v2 phase can never reach 100%
and nobody is asked to decide.

Measured on 2026-10-31 against `accensa/accensa-contracts` at its default branch:

- `docs/ADR-002-upto-scheme.md` still reads **`Status: DRAFT`**, and says in terms: *"Do not cite
  this document as a design that works; cite it as the design being investigated."*
- Of its six questions under "resolve before proposing this upstream", four are still open —
  and they are the structural ones: whether the upstream `upto` wire format allows a
  two-invocation construction at all (§6.1), whether one signed auth entry can cover both
  `authorize` and the nested `approve` (§6.2), sequence-number contention under bursty agent
  traffic (§6.4), and whether the facilitator submits at all (§6.6). §6.3 and §6.5 are marked
  resolved.
- `docs/scheme_upto_stellar.md` — the Stellar scheme spec this design depends on — reads
  **`Status: DRAFT — not yet validated, open proposal for community review`**, and is not
  submitted to the x402 Technical Steering Committee.
- The blockers are closed and the contract is built: `contracts/upto-authorization` exists and
  Accensa issues #64, #65 and #66 are closed. So the *implementation* is ahead of the
  *specification*, which is the one ordering that cannot be settled from our side.

Meanwhile ADR-001's objection to M1 was never answered: the buyer must co-sign settlement, funds
are not locked, and two concurrent M1 pacts from one buyer overwrite each other's SEP-41
allowance. Nothing in the evidence above closes those.

## Decision

1. **Pactwright ships two modes: M0 (exact refund) and M2 (escrow).** M1 is out of the v2 scope.
   P9-06 moves to `[-]` (dropped) with this ADR as its link.
2. **No `upto` surface in our repos.** No vendored `upto-authorization` WASM, no SDK type, no
   verifier condition, no docs example, no facilitator scheme. Verified today: `upto` appears in
   the three repos only in design docs and in the unrelated `PrunedUpTo` ledger-entry symbol in
   Soroban test snapshots.
3. **The metered use case M1 was for is already served by M2.** `close_meter` and signed
   `UsageVoucher`s (P7-01…P7-04) meter usage against a *locked* cap, so per-unit billing does not
   depend on M1 returning. This is the reason dropping M1 costs us no feature.
4. **A concrete re-open trigger, so this is a decision and not a shelving:** M1 is reconsidered
   when *both* hold — Accensa's ADR-002 status line reads **Accepted**, and
   `scheme_upto_stellar.md` is accepted upstream (submitted to and adopted by the x402 TSC).
   Re-opening needs a **new** ADR in this repo that answers ADR-001's three objections; this one
   is not re-opened in place.

## Consequences

- The v2 phase has one fewer unknown, and the integration coverage row for
  `accensa-contracts / upto-authorization` can say `deferred` **with an ADR link**, which is what
  the "Definition of 100% readiness" asks of every source project.
- The contract surface is unchanged: no admin path to funds, both deadlines, one attestation per
  pact, and the 8 invariants stay as they are — nothing here relaxes an essentials rule.
- The `ARCHITECTURE_ESSENTIALS.md` §4 line "M1 is deferred, don't build it" (ADR-002 superseded)
  now points at this ADR too, because "deferred pending someone else's spec" was doing the work of
  a decision.
- Risk we accept: if Accensa accepts `upto` upstream and sellers ask for allowance-based
  payments, we re-enter with a new ADR. The cost of that is one design document, not a migration —
  no pact, receipt or certificate format in this repo references `upto`.
