# ADR-009: Pact storage may archive after a terminal state; TTL covers the settle window

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-28)
- **Governs:** [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) §4.8
- **Review items:** edge case: archived funds

## Context
An open pact whose storage entry archived would leave escrowed funds unreachable until restored.

## Decision
`open` extends the pact entry's TTL past `settle_deadline + GRACE_LEDGERS` and rejects deadlines that need more than the network's maximum TTL. After a terminal state the entry may archive; the anchored receipt is the permanent record.

## Consequences
- `release` can always run inside the window.
- Long-term history comes from receipts, not contract storage.
