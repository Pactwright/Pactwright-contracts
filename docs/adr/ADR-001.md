# ADR-001: Two settlement modes: M0 exact-refund and M2 escrow

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-28)
- **Governs:** [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) §2, §4.9, §5.1–5.3
- **Review items:** O2, B1, B4

## Context
v0.1 proposed three modes. M1 (an allowance through Accensa `upto-authorization`) was weaker than M2 for sellers (the buyer must co-sign settlement and funds are not locked) and more complex than M0. Two concurrent M1 pacts from one buyer also overwrite each other's SEP-41 allowance (B1).

## Decision
Pactwright supports exactly two modes, chosen per seller route:

- **M0 exact-refund:** a standard x402 `exact` payment; DENY or partial delivery refunds through the seller's `RefundVault`. Labelled *refund-promised (seller-cooperative)*.
- **M2 escrow:** the `pact` contract holds the cap; ALLOW pays the seller, DENY/silence/deadline returns funds to the buyer. Guaranteed both ways.

## Consequences
- Every guarantee is stated per mode; M0 is never described as guaranteed.
- M2 needs a facilitator scheme (ADR-004).
- M1 is deferred (ADR-002).
