# ADR-015: Hashing and test vectors owned by pactwright-contracts

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-28)
- **Governs:** [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) §4.3
- **Review items:** B16

## Context
Hashing in two languages and two repos drifts unless one side is authoritative.

## Decision
`contracts/pact/src/hashing.rs` is the source of truth. `scripts/gen_vectors.sh` writes `test-vectors/*.json`; `cargo test` fails on drift; Pactwright-Kit tests against a pinned release of the vectors. `condition_hash.json` is the exception, produced by the Kit (canonical JSON).

## Consequences
- Encoding: `xdr` = ScVal XDR, integers big-endian, ed25519 over the 32-byte digest.
